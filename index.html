Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I’m Devesh, a software engineer in Seattle. I work on customer-facing products and the distributed systems behind them, and I spend some of my free time building small tools that make everyday life a little more useful.

This site is part portfolio, part working notebook—a place to keep track of what I’m building, learning, and thinking about.

A few quick facts

  • I graduated from Georgia Tech in 2023.
  • I work mostly with Java, Python, and TypeScript.
  • I’m interested in distributed systems, production ML, and practical AI products.
  • I like projects that connect software with everyday routines and the physical world.
  • Away from the computer, I’m usually reading, playing soccer or tennis, working out, or cooking.
